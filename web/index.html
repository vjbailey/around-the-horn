    Practice puzzle

    As many as you like. These never affect today's result or your streak.

    How to play

    Connect the two players at the top and bottom of the board through a chain of real teammates.

    • Name anyone who played with the last player in your chain — same club, same season. That's the only rule.
    • Every accepted name shows the team and years they shared.
    • You win when your newest name played alongside the target.
    • Wrong guesses cost nothing. No timer, no guess limit.

    Stuck? The assist button in the search bar lists everyone who played with your last player. It won't tell you which of them reaches the target — that part is the puzzle.

    Your score is simply how many assists you used. Zero is a perfect game. A longer chain doesn't cost you anything, so wander freely.

    One puzzle a day, the same for everyone. Mondays are gentle; it gets harder toward Sunday.
